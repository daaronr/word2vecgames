How to play

  1. The yellow jack is the target word. Your ball starts out as the start word (the dashed circle).
  2. Tap tiles to build a throw: tap once to add a word, again to subtract it, a third time to take it back. Up to three tiles per throw.
    king − man + woman → lands on queen
  3. Throw. Every word is a direction in a 100-dimensional space, so adding and subtracting really moves the ball.
  4. Distance is rank: #12 means the jack is the 12th-nearest word to where your ball stopped. The rings mark the top 10, 100, 1,000 and 10,000. #1 is a bacio, a kiss on the jack.
  5. Four balls per end; your best one counts. After the end you'll see the best throw the hand allowed.

Versus uses real bocce order: whoever is farther from the jack throws next. When the balls run out, the closer side scores a point for each ball that beats the other side's best.
